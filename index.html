How It Works

From ticket to resolved in 4 simple steps

AI handles the heavy lifting. Your team handles the human touch. Customers get fast, accurate answers every time.

1

Ticket Arrives

Customer submits an issue via email, chat, or portal. Lands instantly in your TickoraDesk queue with full context.

2

AI Classifies

AI reads the content, assigns priority, tags it, routes to the right team, and drafts the first response automatically.

3

Agent Responds

Agent opens ticket, reviews AI-drafted reply, edits if needed, and sends in one click. Under 2 minutes total.

4

Issue Resolved

Ticket closed, CSAT collected automatically. AI learns from every interaction to continuously improve quality.
